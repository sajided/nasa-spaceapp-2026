# The Monuments We Left Behind

*An interactive 3D story for ages 6–9 · NASA Space Apps — "Abandoned but not forgotten"*

**How to read this script**

- Every line has a stable **ID** (`s1-01`). The ID is shared by the recording file (`public/audio/<id>.m4a`), the word-timing file and `src/data/story.ts`. Don't renumber lines. If a line is added, give it a suffix such as `s2-04b`.
- `[mood]` sets the speaker's portrait expression. The moods are sad, happy, shocked, annoyed, smiling, curious and proud.
- `(Cue: …)` describes what the 3D scene shows. The mission time (T+) comes from `src/data/separations.ts` and `src/data/mission.ts`.
- 📒 marks the point where a **Monument Album** card is collected. The card's text is in the table at the end.
- Facts marked † should be checked against the Sources section before recording.

## Fact check (changes from the first draft)

- **Arrival:** they reached lunar orbit at about **76 h**, not 100 h. Eagle undocked at 100:12.
- **Ascent stage:** its fate is **unknown**. The first draft said it crashed. NASA lost track of it. A 2021 analysis by James Meador suggests it may still be in lunar orbit.
- **Descent stage:** it lasts "millions of years," not "forever." Micrometeoroids and sunlight slowly wear it down.
- **Interstage ring:** added. It is a separate jettison at about T+ 3:12.
- **S-IC engines:** recovered in 2013 by Bezos Expeditions from about 4.3 km deep in the Atlantic. One was identified as an Apollo 11 engine. They are on display at the Museum of Flight in Seattle.
- **Splashdown:** in the **Pacific** Ocean. *Columbia* is at the Smithsonian National Air and Space Museum.
- **Earth's opening:** Earth no longer laughs at the lonely Moon.
